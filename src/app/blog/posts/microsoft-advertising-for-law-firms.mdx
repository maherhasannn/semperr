Every managing partner knows the Google Ads auction is crowded and expensive. Far fewer have tested the second auction sitting next to it. Microsoft advertising for law firms is one of the few paid search channels where competition is still thin, and where the same keywords can cost meaningfully less per click.

The catch is volume. Microsoft Advertising will not replace Google, and treating it as a replacement is how firms burn budget. Treated as a **supplement with its own targets**, it can lower blended acquisition cost without a new creative or strategy lift.

## Why Microsoft Advertising for Law Firms Deserves a Test

Microsoft Advertising serves ads across Bing, Yahoo, DuckDuckGo-powered results, and the Microsoft Start and Edge ecosystem. Bing holds a modest share of US search overall, but its share is far higher on desktop, where Windows defaults to Edge and Bing.

That matters for legal because of who uses it. The Bing audience skews older and toward desktop users in the workplace. Many of those searchers are researching a serious matter: a wrongful termination, an estate dispute, a business contract problem.

Three structural advantages stand out:

- **Less auction pressure.** Fewer advertisers bid on each keyword, so cost per click is often lower than the same term on Google.
- **Easy import.** You can import existing Google Ads campaigns in minutes and adjust from there.
- **Different intent mix.** Desktop and workplace searchers tend to browse longer and fill out forms rather than call from a phone.

Expect lower volume and treat any claim of "half the cost" as something to verify in your own account. Your market, practice area, and bidding strategy will move the numbers.

## Which Practice Areas Fit Microsoft Advertising for Law Firms

Not every practice area benefits equally. The channel works best where the client is deliberate and the matter is not an emergency.

Strong fits include estate planning, family law, immigration, employment, business litigation, and bankruptcy. Weaker fits include time-critical emergency matters like criminal defense arrests, where mobile search and Google Local Services dominate.

Personal injury sits in the middle. Volume is small, but exclusive-feeling traffic from desktop researchers can convert well on a modest budget. ## Setting Up Your First Campaign

Start by importing, not rebuilding. Connect your Google Ads account in Microsoft Advertising and import your best-performing search campaigns, then review everything before launch.

### What to check after the import

1. **Match types.** Bing treats close variants differently. Confirm that phrase and exact match behave as you expect.
2. **Negative keywords.** Import your negative list and add terms like "free," "jobs," "salary," and "pro bono."
3. **Location settings.** Use "people in your targeted location" rather than the broader option to avoid out-of-state clicks.
4. **Ad copy.** Review every headline against your state bar rules before launch.

### Keep the structure tight

Do not import Performance Max or broad automated campaigns on day one. Start with tightly themed ad groups, and revisit automation once you have conversion data. ## Budgeting and Bid Strategy

Because volume is lower, the budget should be a fraction of your Google spend. A common starting point is 10 to 20 percent of your search budget, run for at least 90 days before judging results.

Give the account enough time to collect conversions. Smart bidding strategies need roughly 30 or more conversions in a cycle to stabilize, and a small account may take months to get there.

Early on, use manual or enhanced CPC and set bids by device. Desktop often deserves a positive bid adjustment for legal, while mobile may need to be reduced depending on your intake setup.

Tie the spend back to economics. If you do not already know your target acquisition cost, work through [how to calculate law firm cost per case](/blog/law-firm-cost-per-case) before setting any bid.

## Compliance, Tracking, and Landing Pages

Microsoft ads are still attorney advertising. Every claim must satisfy your state's rules, which generally track [ABA Model Rule 7.1](https://www.americanbar.org/groups/professional_responsibility/publications/model_rules_of_professional_conduct/rule_7_1_communications_concerning_a_lawyer_s_services/) on false or misleading communications. Avoid guarantees, unverifiable "best" claims, and results language without disclaimers.

Tracking is where most new accounts fail. Install the Universal Event Tracking tag, create conversion goals for calls and form fills, and consider connecting offline conversions so signed cases flow back into the platform. Our guide to [law firm marketing attribution](/blog/law-firm-marketing-attribution) explains how to track which channels actually sign cases.

Finally, send traffic to pages built for the visit. Desktop researchers respond to detailed pages with clear credentials, process steps, and a short form. If your pages underperform, start with the fixes in [law firm landing pages](/blog/law-firm-landing-pages).

## How to Judge Whether the Channel Is Working

Judge Microsoft ads on cost per signed case, not cost per click. A cheap click that never answers the phone is expensive.

Review these metrics monthly:

- Cost per lead and cost per signed case, compared with Google
- Lead-to-consult and consult-to-retained rates
- Share of conversions from desktop versus mobile
- Impression share on your core terms

If cost per signed case sits at or below your Google benchmark after 90 days, scale gradually. If not, pause and check intake speed and landing page quality before blaming the platform. The [Clio Legal Trends Report](https://www.clio.com/resources/legal-trends/) consistently shows that firms responding quickly to inquiries win more clients, and that applies to every lead source.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Microsoft advertising worth it for law firms?

Microsoft advertising is worth testing for most law firms, especially in non-emergency practice areas. Click costs are often lower than Google and competition is lighter, but volume is limited. Run it as a supplement for at least 90 days and judge it on cost per signed case.

### How much should a law firm spend on Bing Ads?

A law firm should typically start with 10 to 20 percent of its Google search budget on Bing Ads. Smaller accounts need time to collect conversion data, so commit to a 90-day window before deciding. Scale only if cost per signed case matches or beats your other channels.

### Can I import my Google Ads campaigns into Microsoft Advertising?

Yes, you can import Google Ads campaigns directly into Microsoft Advertising in a few steps. After importing, review match types, negative keywords, location targeting, and ad copy. Compliance review matters because imported text carries over unchanged.

### Which practice areas perform best on Bing?

Practice areas with deliberate, research-driven clients perform best on Bing. Estate planning, family law, immigration, employment, and business litigation are strong candidates. Emergency matters such as arrests tend to perform better on mobile-heavy channels like Google and Local Services Ads.
