Google keeps pushing advertisers toward Performance Max, and for most industries it works well enough. Performance Max for law firms is a different story, because the campaign optimizes for whatever conversion you hand it, and a legal intake funnel is full of conversions that look good and are worthless.

A form fill from someone searching for a free template counts the same as a signed truck accident case unless you tell Google otherwise. The firms that win with this campaign type are not better at clicking buttons. They are better at feeding the algorithm the right definition of success.

## What Performance Max Actually Does

Performance Max runs a single campaign across Search, YouTube, Display, Discover, Gmail, and Maps. You supply assets (headlines, descriptions, images, video) and audience signals, and Google assembles ads and decides where to show them.

You lose the keyword-level control that makes traditional [law firm PPC advertising](/blog/law-firm-ppc-advertising) predictable. In exchange, Google claims access to inventory you could not reach otherwise.

Here is what that means in practice:

- **Less transparency.** Search term and placement reporting is thinner than in standard Search campaigns.
- **Heavy reliance on conversion data.** The model learns from what you count as a win.
- **Brand cannibalization risk.** Performance Max will happily take credit for people who were already searching your firm name.
- **Broader reach.** Display and YouTube impressions can fill the top of the funnel, but also waste money on irrelevant audiences.

## Why Performance Max for Law Firms Fails So Often

Legal is a high-stakes, low-volume, high-cost-per-click category. A handful of conversions per week is normal, which is far less than the algorithm needs to learn quickly.

The common failure pattern looks like this:

1. The firm sets up a campaign optimizing for form submissions.
2. Google finds the cheapest form submissions, which are often low-intent, out-of-area, or wrong case type.
3. Intake spends its week on unqualified callers.
4. The firm concludes the campaign "doesn't work" and shuts it off.

The campaign did exactly what it was told. The instruction was wrong.

## Fix the Conversion Signal First

Before launching any Performance Max campaign, decide what counts as a conversion. This single decision determines whether automation helps or hurts.

### Move from leads to qualified leads

Import offline conversions from your CRM so Google sees which leads became consultations and signed cases. Even a delayed signal beats a raw form fill, because the model starts to favor traffic patterns that look like your real clients.

If your volume is too low to feed signed-case data, use a middle step: qualified intake call, or a lead that passed your screening criteria. Our guide to [law firm marketing attribution](/blog/law-firm-marketing-attribution) covers how to connect ad clicks to signed cases.

### Value-based bidding

Assign different values to different outcomes. A signed case in a high-value practice area should be worth far more than a general inquiry. Google's bidding then shifts spend toward the traffic that produces value, not volume.

### Filter call conversions

Set a minimum call duration so a hang-up or wrong number is not recorded as a win. Many firms count a 10-second call as a conversion and wonder why their numbers look great while signed cases stay flat.

## Guardrails That Keep Spend Pointed at Cases

Once the signal is sound, add controls. Google gives you fewer levers here, but they exist.

- **Account-level negative keywords.** Block terms like "free," "jobs," "salary," "pro bono," and "how to become a lawyer."
- **Brand exclusions.** Keep brand search in its own campaign so Performance Max cannot inflate its results with your existing demand.
- **Geographic targeting set to "presence."** Exclude "interest" targeting so people merely researching your city are not counted.
- **Customer list exclusions.** Exclude past and current clients from prospecting.
- **Placement exclusions.** Review where Display and YouTube impressions run and cut the junk.

Remember that attorney advertising rules apply to every auto-generated ad. Review our overview of [attorney advertising compliance](/blog/attorney-advertising-compliance) and the [American Bar Association's Model Rule 7.1](https://www.americanbar.org/groups/professional_responsibility/publications/model_rules_of_professional_conduct/rule_7_1_communication_concerning_a_lawyer_s_services/) on misleading communications, because dynamically assembled ad copy still carries your name.

## Build Assets That Signal Case Intent

The algorithm assembles ads from your inputs. Weak assets produce weak ads.

### Write headlines for the situation, not the firm

"Injured in a Truck Crash? Talk to a Lawyer Today" outperforms "Award-Winning Personal Injury Firm" for the person actually searching at 11 p.m. Speak to the problem, the urgency, and the next step.

### Use real video and photography

Performance Max will auto-generate video from your images if you do not supply one. The result is usually generic. A short clip of an attorney explaining what happens after an accident builds more trust, which ties directly to what we outlined in [law firm trust signals](/blog/law-firm-trust-signals).

### Give audience signals, not audience limits

Audience signals are suggestions, not restrictions. Upload customer match lists of signed clients and add custom segments built from the search terms your best cases used. Treat them as a head start for the model.

## How to Test Performance Max for Law Firms Safely

Do not hand over your whole budget. Run a controlled test.

1. **Start with 15 to 20 percent of paid search spend.** Keep your proven Search campaigns running.
2. **Run at least six to eight weeks.** Legal sales cycles are long, and the learning phase takes time.
3. **Compare on cost per signed case, not cost per lead.** This is the only number that matters, and it ties back to your [law firm cost per case](/blog/law-firm-cost-per-case) math.
4. **Check lead quality weekly.** Have intake tag every lead by source and outcome.
5. **Scale only if signed-case economics hold.**

Speed matters as much as targeting. Even a well-tuned campaign wastes money if no one answers, so pair the test with the practices in [speed to lead for law firms](/blog/speed-to-lead-law-firms).

The [Clio Legal Trends Report](https://www.clio.com/resources/legal-trends/) has consistently shown that prospective clients contact multiple firms and often hire the first one that responds. Paid traffic that reaches a slow intake team is traffic you paid for twice.

## Performance Max vs. Search, LSAs, and Lead Purchasing

Performance Max is not a replacement for everything else. It fits a specific role.

- **Standard Search** remains the control layer for high-intent, keyword-specific demand.
- **Local Services Ads** capture ready-to-hire callers at a pay-per-lead price. See our breakdown of [Local Services Ads for law firms](/blog/local-services-ads-for-law-firms).
- **Performance Max** works best as a scaling layer once your conversion data is clean.
- **Purchased exclusive leads** offer predictability when you want volume without managing a black box.

Most firms that scale profitably blend these channels, and measure all of them against the same signed-case yardstick.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Performance Max good for law firms?

Performance Max can work for law firms, but only when fed qualified conversion data. With raw form fills as the goal, it tends to chase cheap, low-intent leads. Firms that import signed-case or qualified-lead data see far better results.

### How much should a law firm spend on a Performance Max campaign?

Start with 15 to 20 percent of your paid search budget and run it for six to eight weeks. Legal campaigns convert slowly and need enough volume to exit the learning phase. Judge success on cost per signed case rather than cost per lead.

### How do I stop Performance Max from wasting budget on junk leads?

Use offline conversion imports, value-based bidding, account-level negative keywords, and a minimum call duration. Also exclude brand terms and past clients so spend goes toward new prospects. Review lead quality with intake every week.

### Does Performance Max replace Google Search campaigns for attorneys?

No, Performance Max supplements Search rather than replacing it. Standard Search campaigns still give you keyword control and clearer reporting for high-intent queries. Most firms keep both and compare results on signed cases.
