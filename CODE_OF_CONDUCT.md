# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Everyone interacting in this repository's issues and pull requests is expected
to be respectful and professional:

- Be considerate and constructive in all communication.
- No harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Assume good faith and focus on what is best for the project.

## Enforcement

Violations may result in comments being removed and offenders being blocked
from the repository. To report unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainers
through the contact form at [semperr.com](https://www.semperr.com).
